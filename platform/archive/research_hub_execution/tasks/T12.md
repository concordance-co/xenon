## Required outputs

C04 typed native sites, dense Qwen3 adapter, head selectors and expert-local schema; native capture/intervention dispatch, capability matrix and tensor oracles. A tiny synthetic MoE fixture verifies routing semantics; real MoE cells remain unqualified unless explicitly demonstrated.

## Implementation sequence

1. Specify tensor axes and meaning for gate preactivation, activated gate, value/up projection and product. For SwiGLU verify product=silu(gate)*up before down projection; never infer this from module names. Preserve signs and record activation function.
2. Support Qwen3-0.6B's exact pinned dense architecture and GPT-2 attention-head output before output projection. Clarify attention probabilities versus per-head vectors, GQA head mapping where applicable and pre/post normalization. Unsupported loci fail during planning.
3. Native edits target a precise tensor quantity; downstream recomputation must match the named intervention. Replacing a gate must recompute product, whereas replacing product leaves the recorded gate unchanged. Support neuron/position selections without writing unselected coordinates.
4. Add expert-local coordinates with selected expert, routed-token mapping and mixture weights. Validate semantics with a tiny explicit MoE; do not treat non-routed tokens as observed zero activations. Real large-MoE support is a later qualification cell, not hidden within dense Qwen acceptance.
5. Preserve row/token alignment and streaming output chunks; avoid materializing all layers/neurons for a simple query. Check read/write sites separately and register capability granularity in planning.

## Verification and acceptance

- Direct-forward and independent tensor reconstruction match every supported quantity at declared precision, including negative gate/value combinations, masked/padded tokens and batched order changes.
- Identity, isolated-coordinate replacement and ablation match explicit rewritten forwards; neighbor neurons/heads remain unchanged at the edit point.
- Hook cleanup, gradient flow, routed-token coverage and unsupported architecture failures tested. Model/site revision mismatch fails before run.
- Real pinned Qwen3-0.6B small capture proves adapter beyond synthetic fixture; Qwen3-32B requires T13 qualification.
