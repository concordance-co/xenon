# Ethical Advantage Vectors

This subproject tests whether Qwen3-30B-A3B has a cleaner polar representation for
`ethical action despite personal cost` versus `self-advantage / exploitative action`
than the earlier named-theory persona-vector setup.

The motivating shift is substrate-first: named moral theories did not yield stable,
specific moral-orientation activations. A sharper pole may exist when the dilemma
directly concerns the actor's own welfare and the ethical action is costly to the
actor.

## Current Phase

- `phase_01`: build and validate a self-interest conflict seed set.

## Design Principle

Every usable dilemma must make three things explicit:

- the actor is the decision-maker;
- the actor personally benefits from the selfish option or personally pays for the
  ethical option;
- the ethical and self-advantage actions are behaviorally distinguishable.
